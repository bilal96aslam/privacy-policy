Help for the Cardora flashcard app

Need help? Email us at its.shopbotics@gmail.com. We reply as soon as we can. Please include the email you signed in with and a short description of the problem.

Common questions

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Settings, tap Manage subscription, then tap Restore purchase. Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used when you bought it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Cardora Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the decks and cards saved to it. It cannot be undone.

My cards or progress are missing

Sign in with the same account you used before. Your decks and progress are backed up to your account and restore when you sign in. If they still do not appear, email us.

Why can I not generate more cards?

Free accounts have a limited number of AI card generations. Cardora Pro removes that limit. Check your internet connection and try again if you see an error.
