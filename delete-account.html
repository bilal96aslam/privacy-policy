Delete Your Cardora Account

This page explains how to permanently delete your Cardora account and the data associated with it. Cardora is developed by Shopbotics.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Cardora app and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account and all associated data are deleted immediately once you confirm. This action cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • Your Google sign-in profile information (name, email, profile photo) as stored by Cardora
  • All of your decks and flashcards
  • Your review history and study statistics
  • Your subscription/usage records tied to your account

What is retained, and for how long

Cardora does not retain any of your personal data after account deletion. Nothing is kept in a backup or retained for any additional period once deletion is confirmed.

Need help?

If you are unable to access the app to delete your account yourself, contact us at its.shopbotics@gmail.com and we will process the deletion on your behalf.
